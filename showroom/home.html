Computers

1987 to now.

My first programs were BASIC on a Brazilian clone of an Apple II. Then C, then C++ as the work grew. Everything since has been either a way of getting closer to the machine or a way of making something other people can use.

These days the work is mostly PHP, Python and JavaScript, plus whatever language a problem forces me to learn. The full list sits on my LinkedIn profile, and I keep it current.
