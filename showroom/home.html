How I got here

I started experimenting with BASIC on a Z80-based MSX, the Sharp HotBit in Brazil, moved on to C and C++ on early x86-based PCs, and eventually into software development during the early days of the web. What began as curiosity became a lifelong technical pursuit.

Over the years I have worked with JavaScript, including React and Vue more recently, Python and SQL, covering both front-end and back-end development, as well as web server environments such as Apache and NGINX. The work has also involved application architecture, database design, API development, system integration and debugging.

I enjoy working across the stack, understanding how components interact, designing systems and turning ideas into working software. The underlying logic of computing interests me most, and I like building applications that are practical, maintainable and well structured.

Beyond conventional software development, since 2005 I have also developed systems in Social Network Analysis (SNA), drawing on cognitive architectures (DARPA's Cougaar), Bayesian networks (SeSAM) and ontologies (Stanford's Protégé), within the broader field of Mathematical Psychology.

More recently I have moved into AI and AI training, evaluating and comparing multiple large language models (LLMs), particularly DeepSeek and MiniMax, and exploring frameworks such as OpenClaw and Hermes for the development and deployment of complex autonomous and multi-agent systems.
